Three things I'd copy from the real one

  • Hide the microphone completely. You cannot see a mic hole on the real device — it's behind that little arc or a pinhole. Ours has an obvious dotted grille. Worth copying: a single small hole under a foam pad looks far better and keeps the wind protection.
  • Make the indicator subtle. They use a white arc, not a glaring LED. You could cover our LED with a frosted window so it glows softly instead of shining — less "gadget", more "jewellery".
  • Aim for 10 mm, not 12. Their thinness is most of why it looks premium. Stack the boards tighter, or use a 3D-printed shell with a thinner wall.

What we genuinely cannot match at ₹500: the seamless machined finish, the four colourways, the custom PCB, the charging dock, and the polished app. Ours will look hand-made — because it will be.
