Nagpur is a metro with a real electronics market. This is the easiest sourcing situation
we've been in — **the two main parts are sitting on shelves in your city right now**, and
one shop delivers in an hour.

numbers assume.

## 3D printing in Nagpur

I couldn't find a dedicated Nagpur print service with published pricing, so:

- Search **JustDial** for "3D printing services, Nagpur" — there are job shops.
- **VNIT Nagpur** and **Government College of Engineering, Nagpur** both have labs with
  3D printers. Students and lab staff are often willing to help with a small part.
- **Makestore** and **Indian Hobby Center** deal in robotics/maker hardware and may print
  or know who does.
- Or upload the file to **Pookie Prints** (Jaipur, ₹4/gram, small prints ₹150–350 incl GST,
  instant quote, ships pan-India).

**Trip 1 — Sitabuldi (one afternoon, everything except the two main parts):**

**Realistic total: ₹330–450** if the small stuff comes from your drawers, ₹600–700 if you

## ⚠️ Before you pay: three things to confirm

1. **Is the "ESP32 C3 Mini" the Super Mini?** You want the small USB-C board, ~22.5 × 18 mm.
   If it's a bigger DevKitM board, the pin numbers in my wiring change.
2. **Is the INMP441 in stock**, not just listed. Ask them to open one and show it.
3. **Verify the 1-hour delivery** applies to your pincode before you rely on it.

## One more thing worth doing

Ask at **Sharma Robot Kits** or **Dangi E Shop** whether they have an **ESP32-C3 with
headers already soldered** — and whether they'd solder your INMP441 to a small board for
a fee. A shop that makes robot kits has the right iron and will do a neat job for ₹50. It
saves you the hardest part of the build and removes the most likely beginner failure.
