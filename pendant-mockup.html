NeoClone pendant — what it will look like

45 × 35 × 12 mm · about 30 g · drawn to scale (4 px = 1 mm)

What the drawing is honest about

  • It's a bit thicker than a phone. 12 mm, because the boards are stacked flat. A 3D-printed shell could shave that to ~10 mm.
  • The button is on the top edge on purpose. Anywhere on the face and your shirt would press it all day.
  • The microphone is off-centre and low. That keeps it away from where the cord attaches, so chain-rub doesn't get recorded.
  • There's no screen, no speaker, no logo light. One LED, one button — which is most of why this costs ₹500 instead of ₹12,000.
  • The first version won't look like this. It'll start as a tangle on a breadboard. The case comes last, only once it works.
